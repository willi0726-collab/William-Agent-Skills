# Competitor Analysis

## Overview

Use this skill to produce source-backed competitor research with clear scope, dates, and actionable takeaways. Separate observed facts from inferred strategy.

## Workflow

1. Define the market, geography, channel, competitors, products, customer segment, and decision the analysis should support.
2. Gather current sources when facts may have changed: official sites, marketplaces, ads libraries, review pages, pricing pages, documentation, and credible third-party data.
3. Capture source dates and URLs. Use screenshots or exports when visual evidence matters.
4. Compare competitors in a matrix: offer, positioning, price, packaging, reviews, traffic/SEO signals, ad angles, strengths, weaknesses, and risks.
5. Convert findings into prioritized recommendations with evidence, expected impact, and confidence.

## Output Standards

Include:

- Scope and source list
- Competitor matrix
- Key differences
- Opportunities
- Threats
- Recommended actions
- Confidence and unknowns

## Guardrails

Do not present stale or uncited market facts as current. Do not fabricate revenue, traffic, ad spend, or sales estimates. Label estimates and explain the method.
